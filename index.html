In which Indian states should a company open its next distribution hubs for higher growth?

Key finding
Chhattisgarh and Andhra Pradesh are the best hub picks, with forecast amount growth of 18.9% / 16.5%, low volatility, and per capita goods gains beating GSDP growth (95% vs 47% for Chhattisgarh).
Method
Time series forecasting, classification and KMeans clustering on 33 states, using 7 years of E-Way Bill data.
Why it matters
It points a company to the states where hub growth looks both strong and steady.
Finance

The IPO Reality Check

PythonMySQL
Question
Invested on the same day, which earns more in 1 year: a newly listed stock or Nifty50 stocks?
Method
Cohort analysis, Spearman correlation and OLS regression on 294 IPOs from 2019-2025.
Finding
Nifty50 earned more: median 1-year returns were -5.4% (high listing-day jump IPOs) and 0.4% (low jump IPOs), against about 8% for Nifty50.

Retail

Festival Restocking Problem

PythonMySQL
Question
Before major festivals, which states should a retailer restock, and how many weeks early?
Method
Lead-lag analysis, KMeans clustering and backtesting on weekly search-interest data across 15 states.
Finding
Demand starts building anywhere from 0-1 to 10+ weeks before a festival, and the 15 states form 4 regional groups with different timing for the same festival.

Public Health

Suicide Patterns in India

Power BI
Question
Do clear suicide patterns exist among specific groups and regions in India?
Method
4-page Power BI dashboard on 5 years (2018-2022) of data across state, cause, job, education and income.
Finding
Top groups were daily wage earners, Class 10 educated, and under 1 lakh income. Top cause: family problems (32%).
